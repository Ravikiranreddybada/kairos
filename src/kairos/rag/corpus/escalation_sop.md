# Stockout Escalation SOP

Owner: Store Operations.

## Severity levels

Severity 1: an A-class SKU is out of stock in more than one store, or stockout probability above 50% within lead time.
Severity 2: a single-store stockout of an A or B-class SKU.
Severity 3: C-class or intermittent SKU stockout.

## Response times

Severity 1 must be escalated to the regional supply manager within 2 hours, and an expedited order raised the same day.
Severity 2 must be resolved by the next scheduled order cycle; store transfers are preferred over expedited freight.
Severity 3 is reviewed in the weekly planning meeting.

## Forecast anomalies

When an unexplained demand spike above 4 robust standard deviations is detected, planners must confirm whether it is a data error before changing orders.
Data-entry errors are corrected in the source system, never overridden in the forecast.
