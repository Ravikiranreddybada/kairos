# Promotion Playbook

Owner: Commercial. Rules for planning and evaluating promotions.

## Planning a promotion

Promotions run 3 to 7 days. Standard price discount is 15%.
Stores must pre-build stock to cover forecast uplift plus safety stock at least one supplier lead time before the start date.
Do not run promotions on SKUs from suppliers with on-time rate below 85% without a confirmed delivery slot.

## Measuring promotion impact

Incremental volume is promo-period sales minus the forecast baseline without promotion.
Always subtract the post-promotion dip (pull-forward), typically 3 days at about 20% below baseline.
A promotion is profitable when incremental margin exceeds the discount cost on baseline volume.

## Cannibalisation

Promotions on one SKU can reduce sales of substitutes in the same category by 5 to 15%.
Category managers must review cannibalisation for any promotion above 40% expected uplift.
